# Carbinox performance-led email copy rules

These rules come from a read-only review of the 50 highest attributed-revenue campaigns in a two-year Klaviyo report. The review combined 678 sent-email report rows. It ranks by Shopify Placed Order attributed revenue and includes related sends/clones; the findings are patterns in winners, not controlled evidence that a specific copy choice caused revenue. The source export and machine-readable rankings stay in the ignored `private-data/` directory because this GitHub repository is public.

## Voice and structure

- Use plain-spoken, casual, confident language centered on practical usefulness and hard-wearing product value.
- Address the reader directly. Keep paragraphs and sentences short; fragments can work where they sound natural.
- Give each email one main product, offer, or story. Lead with one hook and explain the value clearly.
- Use one dominant, direct CTA. The reviewed emails use verbs such as Shop, Get, Claim, Unlock, See, and Start.
- Keep preheaders optional. Many winners had no substantive preheader; when present, add a benefit or deadline rather than echoing the subject.

## Performance-backed angles to rotate

- **Launch and offer:** the top six sends are an Edge launch/relaunch and specific Black Friday offers.
- **Trade-in value:** the winner set includes repeated trade-in announcements and reminders that explain the old watch's value.
- **Early access and milestones:** anniversary and access-window sends appear repeatedly in the top 50.
- **Seasonal value:** seasonal promotions and gift/bundle value also appear.
- **Product proof:** practical utility and durability language appears throughout the body-copy sample.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Never invent discounts, reference prices, stock counts, deadlines, guarantees, testimonials, review counts, specifications, or social-proof numbers.
- Treat Shopify's current listed price as a catalog price, not a discount.
- Use exact scarcity or offer claims only when someone supplies them as verified current facts.
- The social-proof footer varies between 350,000+ and 400,000+ in reviewed templates. Do not reuse it automatically.
- Avoid vague legacy filler such as “spectacular offer” unless the actual offer is stated.

## How to evaluate performance

Absolute attributed revenue is useful for identifying commercially important sends but favors larger audiences. Compare revenue per recipient and click rate alongside revenue. Repeated or cloned sends are not independent creative tests, and the current data does not prove causation.
